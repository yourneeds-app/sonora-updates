# Sonora

**Your audiobooks. Your moment.** An Android app for your Audiobookshelf library, by [Your Needs](https://yourneeds.app).

[Download the latest version](https://github.com/yourneeds-app/sonora-updates/releases/latest) · [Discover Sonora](https://sonora.yourneeds.app)

You need an accessible Audiobookshelf server and access to your library. For most current Android phones and tablets, choose the **arm64-v8a APK** and install it over your existing Sonora installation. Updates are also available through the app.

Sonora is a fork of **Absorb**. The current release includes its corresponding source ZIP, licence notices and SHA-256 checksums.

## Your audiobooks. Beyond your headphones.

Stream from Sonora on Android to compatible **AirPlay 2 speakers**, including selected **Sonos** speakers tested in practice. Open Cast, choose an available speaker and adjust its volume in Sonora. You can also switch to another available speaker.

Compatibility depends on the speaker model, firmware and network. Our tests do not establish support for every speaker or every AirPlay 2 feature, including synchronised multiroom playback.

### Deine Hörbücher. Auch auf deinen Lautsprechern.

Streame aus Sonora auf Android zu kompatiblen **AirPlay-2-Lautsprechern**, darunter ausgewählte **Sonos**-Lautsprecher aus unseren Praxistests. Öffne Cast, wähle einen verfügbaren Lautsprecher und regle seine Lautstärke in Sonora. Du kannst auch zu einem anderen verfügbaren Lautsprecher wechseln.

Die Kompatibilität hängt von Modell, Firmware und Netzwerk ab. Unsere Tests belegen keine Unterstützung sämtlicher Lautsprecher oder aller AirPlay-2-Funktionen, etwa synchroner Multiroom-Wiedergabe.
